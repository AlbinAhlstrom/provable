How to play
  • Clear every square that does not hide a mine. A number tells how many of the eight squares around it are mines.
  • Mouse: left-click digs, right-click plants or removes a flag. Click a number (or middle-click it) once it has that many flags around it to dig the rest of its neighbours.
  • Touch: tap digs, press and hold flags. Or switch to Flag mode so a tap flags and a hold digs.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter or Space digs, F flags, R or F2 starts a new game.
  • Your first dig is always safe and always opens an area.
